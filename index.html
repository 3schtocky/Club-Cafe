Our Story

A Pittsburgh Landmark Since 1999

Club Cafe is a renowned, intimate live music venue and iconic staple of Pittsburgh's historic South Side district. The intimate space is host to a nightly lineup of touring acts from all over the world as well as local artists from Pittsburgh's thriving music scene.

Founded in 1999 by Marco and Paula Cardamone in an intimate space on Bedford Square, Club Cafe quickly became Pittsburgh's premier destination for discovering extraordinary talent before they hit the big stage.

Pittsburgh landmark Club Cafe is the best club to see live music in the city, also serving an exclusive list of cocktails, drafts, cans, wine, and small plates.

Club Cafe Coffee

Bringing a fresh, creative energy to Pittsburgh's South Side. Our Coffee House serves Ghost Coffee Collab beans roasted by Trace Brewery, alongside a curated menu of specialty coffees, teas, and light bites.

Enjoy local bakery items and breakfast sandwiches from Pittsburgh Sandwich Society. Stop in and grab a coffee (or a chocolate milk) and a sandwich before your day begins.
