Our Story

A Pittsburgh Landmark Since 1999

Club Cafe is a renowned, intimate live music venue and iconic staple of Pittsburgh's historic South Side district. The intimate space is host to a nightly lineup of touring acts from all over the world as well as local artists from Pittsburgh's thriving music scene.

The story began in the spring of 1999, when founders Marco and Paula Cardamone opened a performance space on Bedford Square in a building with deep South Side roots — dating back to the 1930s when it was a "shot and a beer" watering hole for Pittsburgh's steelworkers. Club Cafe created exceptional acoustics within a small, intimate environment designed to be the most intimate music performance stage in Pittsburgh.

For 25 years, the 125-seat space served as a launchpad where future stars cut their teeth, Pittsburgh artists played their first sets, and countless late-night encores turned strangers into regulars.

In 2025, Danielle Mashuda and Maddy Lafferty of Keystone Artist Connect stepped in to carry Club Cafe forward — bringing a combined 24 years of entertainment industry experience, a vision for community, and a deep love for Pittsburgh's music scene. Joined by partners Read Connolly, Kristen Whitlinger, and Elliott Sussman, they reopened the doors with a refreshed interior, expanded food and drink offerings, and a promise to stay true to the club's acoustic roots while broadening the range of programming.

Under new ownership, Club Cafe is more than a performance space — it's a hangout. Folk, Americana, and singer-songwriter acts share the stage with funk and jazz jams, punk shows, hip-hop sets, drag revues, and LGBTQ DJ dance nights. On nights without ticketed shows, the doors stay open for community programming: open mics, DJ sets, trivia, karaoke, and whatever else gets people in.

Mornings

Club Cafe Coffee

Bringing a fresh, creative energy to Pittsburgh's South Side. Our Coffee House serves Ghost Coffee Collab beans roasted locally by Trace Brewing — one of the city's favorites.

Enjoy breakfast sandwiches from Pittsburgh Sandwich Society (operating out of Strange Roots Taproom in Millvale), fresh pastries from Prantl's Bakery, and a curated menu of specialty coffees, teas, and light bites.

Stop in and grab a coffee (or a chocolate milk) and a sandwich before your day begins.
